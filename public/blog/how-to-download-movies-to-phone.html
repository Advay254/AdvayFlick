What You Need

  • Any Android phone (Android 6.0 or newer)
  • SPlayer — free torrent-streaming app (Play Store)
  • A stable internet connection (Wi-Fi recommended for 1080p+)

Step by Step

Step 1 — Install SPlayer

Search for SPlayer on the Google Play Store and install it. It's free, lightweight, and handles magnet links natively.

Step 2 — Find your movie on SkyluxMovies

Open SkyluxMovies in your browser and search for any movie. Browse trending, top rated, or search by title.

Step 3 — Choose quality

On the movie detail page, choose your quality. For phone storage, 720p (~800MB) is ideal. If you have space and a fast connection, 1080p (~1.5GB) gives noticeably better quality.

Step 4 — Tap Download

Tap Download. Android will ask which app to open the magnet link with — select SPlayer. The download starts immediately and saves to your phone storage.

Step 5 — Watch offline

Once downloaded, the movie plays in SPlayer without any internet connection. Perfect for flights, road trips, or areas with poor signal.

How Much Storage Do Movies Use?

  • 720p — ~700MB–1GB per movie
  • 1080p — ~1.2GB–2GB per movie
  • 4K (2160p) — ~4GB–8GB per movie

Can I Download on Mobile Data?

Yes, but we recommend Wi-Fi for anything above 720p. A 1080p movie will use roughly 1.5GB of mobile data.
