Problem 1 — Nothing Happens When I Tap Download

Cause

The download button opens a magnet link. If no app on your phone handles magnet links, nothing will happen.

Fix

Install SPlayer from the Google Play Store. On first tap, Android will ask which app to use — select SPlayer. Future taps will open automatically.

Problem 2 — Download Starts But Stalls at 0%

Cause

No seeders available for that particular torrent at that moment, or your internet connection is blocking torrent traffic.

Fix

  • Try a different quality — if 1080p stalls, try 720p which usually has more seeders
  • Wait a few minutes and retry — seeder availability fluctuates
  • Switch from mobile data to Wi-Fi — some carriers throttle torrent traffic

Problem 3 — Download Is Very Slow

Cause

Few active seeders, or network throttling by your ISP or carrier.

Fix

  • Use a VPN — this bypasses ISP throttling and typically improves torrent speeds significantly
  • Choose 720p instead of 1080p — smaller file, more seeders, faster download
  • Download during off-peak hours (late night) when more seeders are active

Problem 4 — Film Plays But Has No Audio

Cause

The video uses an audio codec your player doesn't support natively (commonly TrueHD or Atmos).

Fix

Switch to VLC for Android — it supports virtually every audio codec including TrueHD, DTS, and Dolby Atmos. Free on the Play Store.

Problem 5 — Film Downloaded But Won't Play

Cause

Incomplete download — the torrent didn't finish before the app closed, or the file is corrupted.

Fix

Delete the partial file, reopen the magnet link, and allow the download to complete fully before playing. SPlayer shows a progress indicator — wait for 100%.

Problem 6 — SkyluxMovies Site Won't Load

Cause

Render free tier may be spinning up after inactivity (takes ~30 seconds on first load).

Fix

Wait 30 seconds and refresh. After the first load the site stays responsive for hours.
